About Me

Hello! I'm Sagar Kotrangi, a passionate and detail-driven graduate in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, dedicated to continuous learning and growth in the field of technology.

My academic journey began with a strong foundation in engineering, completed with a Diploma from Government Polytechnic College, Nirmal, and later a Bachelor's degree from St. Mary's Group of Institutions, Hyderabad. During these formative years, I immersed myself in not only theoretical knowledge but also hands-on project development — experiences that shaped my interest in practical innovation, automation, and system design.

I believe technology is most powerful when it's used to solve real-world problems. That belief led me to develop projects such as a Soil Moisture-Based Irrigation System and an Alcohol Sensing Engine Lock System, both designed to improve safety and efficiency in daily life. These experiences sharpened my problem-solving mindset and taught me how to think critically, collaborate with others, and adapt to challenges under time constraints.

While my core academic background is in Electrical Engineering, I've always been drawn to software and data. I've taken the initiative to explore technologies like Python, Pandas, NumPy, and MS Excel through various online certifications. I also earned the prestigious IBM Data Science Professional Certificate, which deepened my understanding of data analysis, visualization, and applied machine learning.

I am fascinated by the intersection of hardware and software — where sensors meet scripts, where devices learn from data, and where machines communicate intelligently. This drives my growing interest in IoT, automation, and data-driven solutions. I’m also constantly working to improve my soft skills, such as communication, leadership, and teamwork, to be not just a great engineer, but a valuable team player.

My short-term goal is to begin my career in a dynamic organization where I can apply my technical and analytical skills. My long-term goal is to evolve into a solution architect or data-driven engineer — someone who not only builds systems, but improves them intelligently using technology and data insights.

Outside of academics and technology, I’m a curious learner, an occasional writer, and someone who believes in the power of perseverance, simplicity, and focus.

Thank you for taking the time to read about my journey. I look forward to learning, building, and contributing to the tech community — one challenge at a time.
